YARD WARS

A fast top-down arena shooter. Pick a rowdy, grab the coins, win the yard.

Install Meet the rowdies Source code

Free, no ads, no accounts. Runs on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.

What is it?

Yard Wars is a small arcade shooter seen from above. You run around a yard full of walls, crates and bushes, aim with the right stick and let go to fire. Hits charge your super, a special attack that can turn a fight around.

Hide in bushes to ambush enemies, pick up medpacks after a knockout, and smash the treasure chests that drop into the arena: they spill coins. Coins unlock new rowdies.

Modes

Duel

You against a bot. First to 10 knockouts wins, or whoever has more after 3 minutes.

Team fight

3 against 3: you and two bots against three bots. First team to 15 knockouts.

Waves

Survive! Every wave brings one more bot. You have 3 lives.

Play with friends

All modes also work over your Wi-Fi: one device hosts, the others join. The host is found automatically. Duel becomes a free-for-all, team fight puts all players in one team against the bots, and waves becomes co-op.

Yard Pass

Every round earns XP: for playing, winning, knockouts and broken chests. XP fills the tiers of the current season - 30 of them, each with a reward to claim: coins, a new rowdy, skins, a bullet trail, a pedestal for the lobby, a badge and a title. After the last tier, more XP keeps bringing bonus coins.

Three challenges and a big challenge give extra XP (swap one you don't like). Take your time: they wait until you finish them, and then a new one comes. The pass is completely free: there is no paid track and nothing to buy. Nothing expires, and taking a break never costs you anything - switch back to an older season and finish it whenever you like. Your progress stays on your device, like your coins.

Season 1 is Garden Party: the new rowdy, the Gardener, at tier 10, rose, sunflower, tulip and lavender skins, a leaf trail, a flower pot pedestal and the title "Yard Veteran".

Install

Yard Wars runs on LÖVE, a free game engine. You install LÖVE once, then add the game. After that the game updates itself: every start it checks for a new version, downloads it and switches over.

Android

  1. Install LÖVE for Android 11.5: download love-11.5-android.apk from the LÖVE releases page and open it (allow installing apps from your browser when asked). Start LÖVE once, then close it.
  2. On a computer, download yard-wars.zip and unzip it.
  3. Connect the phone with a USB cable and choose File transfer on the phone.
  4. On the phone's storage, open Android/data/org.love2d.android/files/, create the folders games/lovegame and copy the unzipped files into lovegame (main.lua must be directly inside it).
  5. Start LÖVE from its icon: Yard Wars starts.

Opening the file directly from a download or cloud app doesn't work with LÖVE for Android, so the USB copy is needed once. Later versions arrive by themselves.

Windows, macOS, Linux

  1. Install LÖVE 11.5 from love2d.org (Linux: also available in most package managers as love).
  2. Download yard-wars.love.
  3. Double-click it, or run love yard-wars.love.

Playing over Wi-Fi

All devices must be in the same network. If others can't find or join your game, allow UDP ports 27015 and 27016 in your firewall (Linux with ufw: sudo ufw allow 27015:27016/udp).

Controls

Touch

  • Left half: move (the stick appears where you touch)
  • Right half: drag to aim, release to fire; drag back to the centre to cancel
  • Quick tap: fires at the nearest enemy
  • Super button: same as the aim stick, glows when your super is ready

Keyboard and mouse

  • W A S D: move
  • Mouse: aim, hold the left button to fire
  • Right button or E: aim the super, release to fire
  • M: sound on/off, Esc: back to the lobby
